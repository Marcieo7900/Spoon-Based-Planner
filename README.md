---

## 💜 What Is This?

The **Spoon-Based Meal & Movement Planner** is a compassionate web application designed for people managing chronic pain, mental health challenges, or just... life. Based on [Christine Miserandino's Spoon Theory](https://www.nchpad.org/resources/spoon-theory-a-new-way-to-think-about-your-daily-energy/), this tool generates personalized meal and movement suggestions based on your energy level (1–5 spoons), dietary needs, pain points, and movement preferences.

**No guilt. No pressure. Just gentle guidance for where you are right now.**

---

## ✨ Features

### 🍽️ Smart Meal Planning

* **160+ recipes** across breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks
* **12 dietary filters**: Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free, Keto, Paleo, Mediterranean, DASH, Low-Sodium, Diabetic, Low-Calorie, and more
* **Spoon-level matching**: Get meals appropriate for your energy level AND easier options
* **Partial match support**: Shows best matches even when exact combinations aren't available

### 💪 Pain-Aware Movement

* **8 workout categories**: Flexibility, Balance, Cardio, Strength, HIIT, Circuit, Functional, or "Surprise Me"
* **Pain filtering**: Uses your selected pain areas to avoid exercises flagged for those areas when possible
* **Intensity scoring**: Each exercise rated 1–5 spoons
* **Lower-level inclusion**: Like meals, shows options from your level and below

### 💬 Fandom-Inspired Affirmations

* **100+ affirmations** referencing Doctor Who, Marvel, Supernatural, Harry Potter, Lucifer, Wednesday, Sailor Moon, and more
* **Makeup-positive messaging**: Beauty as self-expression, not obligation
* **Mental health aware**: Supportive messages for difficult mental health days
* **Body neutral**: Weight gain isn't a moral failure

## 🚀 How to Use

1. **Clone or download** this repository
2. **Open `index.html`** in any modern web browser
3. **Select your options**:

   * How many spoons do you have today? (1–5)
   * Any pain points?
   * Dietary needs?
   * Meal type and movement preference
4. **Click "Generate My Plan"**
5. **Receive** meal, movement, and affirmation suggestions based on the options you selected

**No internet required** after download — the planner works client-side!

## ⚕️ Wellness Disclaimer

The **Spoon-Based Meal & Movement Planner** is intended for general wellness, educational, and informational purposes only. It is **not a substitute for professional medical, nutritional, mental health, or individualized fitness advice, diagnosis, or treatment.**

Meal, movement, spoon-level, pain-area, and affirmation suggestions are general recommendations and may not be appropriate for every person, health condition, dietary need, injury, disability, or mobility level.

Always listen to your body. Stop any movement that causes pain or makes existing symptoms worse, and consult a qualified healthcare professional when appropriate before making significant changes to your diet, exercise, or wellness routine.

The planner is designed to help you work with the capacity you have today — **rest is always a valid option.**

## 💚 Why I Built This

Marcevolution isn't just a brand—it's a safe space for real people navigating real struggles. As someone who lives with chronic pain, depression, anxiety, and PTSD, I wanted to create something that meets people where they are.

No toxic positivity. No "just try harder." No treating a low-spoon day like a failed day.

Some days you may have the capacity to cook a full meal and move your body. Other days, making the easiest thing available and resting may be exactly what you need.

The goal isn't to make you perform for the planner. **The planner is here to work with you.**

**You're not behind in life. You're on your own timeline.**
