# 🥄 Spoon-Based Meal & Movement Planner

> *Gentle meals & movement for where you're at today*

**By [Marcevolution](https://marceshia.wixsite.com/home)** | A spoonie-friendly wellness tool for tired humans

---

## 💜 What Is This?

The **Spoon-Based Meal & Movement Planner** is a compassionate web application designed for people managing chronic pain, mental health challenges, or just... life. Based on [Christine Miserandino's Spoon Theory](https://www.nchpad.org/resources/spoon-theory-a-new-way-to-think-about-your-daily-energy/), this tool generates personalized meal and exercise recommendations based on your energy level (1-5 spoons), dietary needs, pain points, and movement preferences.

**No guilt. No pressure. Just gentle guidance for where you are right now.**

---

## ✨ Features

### 🍽️ Smart Meal Planning
- **160+ recipes** across breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks
- **12 dietary filters**: Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free, Keto, Paleo, Mediterranean, DASH, Low-Sodium, Diabetic, Low-Calorie, and more
- **Spoon-level matching**: Get meals appropriate for your energy level AND easier options
- **Partial match support**: Shows best matches even when exact combinations aren't available

### 💪 Pain-Safe Movement
- **8 workout categories**: Flexibility, Balance, Cardio, Strength, HIIT, Circuit, Functional, or "Surprise Me"
- **Pain filtering**: Automatically excludes exercises that would aggravate your specific pain points (back, knees, hands, neck)
- **Intensity scoring**: Each exercise rated 1-5 spoons
- **Lower-level inclusion**: Like meals, shows options from your level and below

### 💬 Fandom-Inspired Affirmations
- **100+ affirmations** referencing Doctor Who, Marvel, Supernatural, Harry Potter, Lucifer, Wednesday, Sailor Moon, and more
- **Makeup-positive messaging**: Beauty as self-expression, not obligation
- **Mental health aware**: Depression, anxiety, and PTSD validation
- **Body neutral**: Weight gain isn't moral failure

---

## 🚀 How to Use

1. **Clone or download** this repository
2. **Open `index.html`** in any modern web browser
3. **Select your options**:
   - How many spoons do you have today? (1-5)
   - Any pain points?
   - Dietary needs?
   - Meal type and movement preference
4. **Click "Generate My Plan"**
5. **Receive** 3 meal options and curated exercises + a random affirmation

**No internet required** after download - works completely client-side!
